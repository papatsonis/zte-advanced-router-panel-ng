v2026-ng1.38: IPv6 DNS, checked cell locks, sturdier reconnect

- Manual DNS takes IPv6 too (primary and secondary). In the IPv6 prompt,
  keep the values shown to keep them; empty or "-" means no IPv6 DNS;
  "-" in one place clears just that one.
- Cell locks: what you type is checked before anything is sent (PCI and
  channel ranges; for 5G, that the channel belongs to the band). A 5G
  cell lock is read back to confirm the router stored it.
- Guided LTE cell lock: if the router does not switch to LTE only,
  nothing is locked and the previous mode is put back.
- Reconnect: when the router refuses the last step while the page is
  still logged in, the panel checks whether data is already back and
  otherwise tries again in the page.
- The panel says when the router has just restarted.
- Lighter polling (one request at a time); fixed the built-in SHA-256
  used on plain-http pages for some input lengths; n77/n78 channels are
  labelled as such in the neighbor scan; Esc closes dialogs; smaller
  minimised panel on phones; five clicks on the footer switch on
  verbose console logging.
- tools/build_lite.py: the 5G probe's Restore button may call
  send_nr_bands; only its definition must stay outside the removed block.
- README: features, things to know, compatibility (1.38 checks on all
  three routers) and version history.
